Retail Sales Data Analysis Using Excel

Project Overview

This beginner-level data analytics project analyzes fictional retail sales transactions using Microsoft excel. The objective is to summarize sales performance , compare product and regional revenue , and present findings through a dashboard.

Business Objectives
- Calculate total revenue , product cost, gross profit, and gross profit margin.
- Identify the highest- revenue product.
- compare sales performance across regions.
- Present key performance indicators (KPIs) in a visual dashboard.

Tools Used
-Microsoft Excel
-Excel formulas: SUM , SUMIF , COUNTIF, COUNTA
-sorting , Filtering , data-quality checks, charts, and KPI cards

Dataset
-The practice dataset contains 12 fictional retail transactions across four products and four regions . Fields include order ID, date , product , region , quantity , unit price, and unit cost.

Key Findings
- Total sales revenue: 359900
- Total product cost: 289050
- Gross profit: 70850
- Gross profit margin: approximately 19.69%
- Laptop was the highest- revenue product , generating 250000
- East was the highest-revenue region , generating 124500

Methodology
1. Organised and reviewed the source dataset.
2. Checked order ID and data completeness.
3. Calculated transaction-level sales, cost and profit.
4. Summarized results by product and region using SUMIF.
5. Created KPI cards, charts and business findings excel.
6. Validated dashboard totals against the underlying data.

Business Recommendations
- Monitor laptop inventory and demand.
- Investigate factors associated with the East region's higher revenue.
- Track gross profit margin over time before making pricing or cost decisions.

Limitations
- This project uses a small , fictional dataset for learning purposes. the findings are illustrative and should not be treated as conclusions about an actual business.

How to Use

Download Sales_Data_Analysis.xlsx and open it in Microsoft Excel to explore the source data, product and regional summaries, and dashboard.
